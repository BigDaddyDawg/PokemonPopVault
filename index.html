Add to Home Screen

iPhone doesn’t show an Install button like Android — use Safari’s Share menu instead.

  1. Open this site in Safari (not Chrome or Instagram’s browser).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Afterwards it opens full screen like an app, with your owned list and wishlist synced.
